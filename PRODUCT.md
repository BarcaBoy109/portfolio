## Themes and interaction

Automatic (season & rain) is the default theme setting. It uses Singapore's calendar season and checks NEA rainfall observations every five minutes. Current rainfall selects Raining; unavailable or stale data falls back to the seasonal theme. A manually chosen theme or a personal celebration date takes precedence.

Annual celebration dates: January 5, March 24, May 2, May 16, July 4, July 13, August 18, and November 12.

Project screenshots can activate embedded previews. Each project also has a direct link. Weather animation can be paused and follows reduced-motion preferences.
